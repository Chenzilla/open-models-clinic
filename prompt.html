The brief

The identical prompt sent to every model. Single request, no system prompt, no tools, vendor-default reasoning, no output cap.

Build a visually memorable, interactive healthcare website called "Care, in motion."

The experience follows one fictional patient through a clinic visit. Its centerpiece is a large, custom illustrated clinic that visitors can explore. The audience includes clinicians and people with little technical experience: the experience should be understandable immediately and interesting enough to demonstrate on a large screen.

Deliver one complete, self-contained HTML document with embedded CSS and JavaScript. Return only the HTML, starting with <!DOCTYPE html>, without Markdown fences, commentary, or explanation before or after it.

Use plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. No external libraries, fonts, images, CDNs, network requests, or tools. Create the visual assets yourself using inline SVG, CSS, or canvas. The page must work offline.

VISUAL DIRECTION

Create a sophisticated architectural illustration brought to life.

Use a warm ivory background, dark ink typography, restrained teal accents, and a contrasting accent for the patient's active location. Choose the exact colors yourself. Combine expressive display typography with highly readable interface text, using available system fonts.

The main visual must be a large, detailed isometric or axonometric cutaway of a fictional clinic. It should occupy at least half of the opening desktop viewport.

Draw recognizable spaces:

* An entrance and reception desk.
* A waiting area with individual chairs and plants.
* A vitals station with a chair and equipment.
* A consultation room with a desk, chairs, and examination table.
* A checkout area.

Make the clinic feel spatially coherent through consistent perspective, walls, floor surfaces, furniture, depth, shadows, and lighting. Include small details that reward a closer look. Keep routes and labels legible.

The patient must be a recognizable illustrated marker or character whose location can move through the clinic. The route should follow the walkable floor between spaces. Rooms should respond visually when active or selected.

Design the whole page as a cohesive experience, including the header, typography, controls, explanatory text, and footer. Use the headline "A clearer picture of every visit." Write concise supporting copy appropriate to a fictional clinic walkthrough.

THE INTERACTIVE STORY

Follow a fictional patient named Jamie through these five milestones:

1. 9:00 AM — Arrival
Location: Entrance
Detail: Jamie arrives for a scheduled appointment.
2. 9:03 AM — Check-in
Location: Reception
Detail: Contact information and visit paperwork are confirmed.
3. 9:10 AM — Vitals
Location: Vitals station
Detail: A care-team member records measurements and reviews the medication list.
4. 9:18 AM — Consultation
Location: Consultation room
Detail: Jamie and the clinician discuss the reason for the visit and agree on next steps.
5. 9:35 AM — Checkout
Location: Checkout
Detail: Jamie receives the visit summary and arranges follow-up.

Display an interactive timeline with these exact times and milestones. The following elements must stay synchronized:

* The selected timeline milestone.
* The patient's location in the clinic.
* The highlighted room or area.
* The displayed time.
* A concise detail panel.
* Elapsed visit time measured from 9:00 AM.

Users must be able to:

* Select any timeline milestone, including moving backward.
* Select a corresponding room in the illustration to jump to its milestone.
* Press Play to advance through the journey, with one milestone every three seconds.
* Pause at the current milestone.
* Restart at Arrival.

Animate the patient traveling along the route and transition room highlights smoothly. Playback must stop at Checkout. Start paused at Arrival so the initial view is consistent.

Below the main experience, include an elegantly designed visit-summary section showing all five milestones and the total visit duration. Make it feel like part of the same visual story.
